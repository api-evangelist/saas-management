SaaS Management

SaaS Management covers the tools, platforms, and practices used by organizations to discover, manage, optimize, and secure their portfolio of SaaS applications. The global SaaS management platform market is valued at approximately $16.85 billion in 2026 and projected to reach $35.1 billion by 2035. Key capabilities include SaaS discovery (shadow IT), license optimization, spend management, access control, security posture management, and compliance monitoring. The average enterprise manages over 300 SaaS applications, driving demand for centralized management platforms with open APIs for integration with ITSM, HRIS, and identity providers.
